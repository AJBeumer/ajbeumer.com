## Areas of depth

Designing schemas that serve editorial teams without creating maintenance nightmares. Getting the structure right before anyone writes a line of configuration.

**Custom plugin development**  
Extending CMS platforms where out-of-the-box functionality stops short of what editors actually need.

**Editorial workflow design**  
Mapping business publishing processes into software workflows. Knowing when a field is a UI decision and when it's an architectural one.

**Headless & structured content**

**Multi-channel publishing**  
Content that needs to exist in more than one form — web, app, print-ready — requires a content model that doesn't assume the output format.
